Our Mission

When a pipe bursts at 2am, when water is rising in the basement, when fire has damaged your home — you don't need 14 tabs open and 7 phone calls to find someone who answers. You need the right licensed professional in your city, immediately.

HomeServiceDirectory was built to solve that problem at national scale. We connect homeowners with verified, licensed, insured home service contractors in every city and state in America — for every category of home emergency and repair service.

What We Do

HomeServiceDirectory operates a national paid listing directory across 12 home service categories. Homeowners use the platform to find, compare, and contact licensed local professionals. Businesses use it to reach homeowners actively looking for their services.

Trust & Transparency

HomeServiceDirectory is committed to transparency in how we operate and how we present providers.

  • Provider listing status (paid vs. free) is visible on all profiles
  • Trust Scores are calculated algorithmically — they cannot be purchased
  • Reviews are submitted by homeowners, not generated by providers
  • License information is submitted by providers — we encourage homeowners to independently verify with state licensing boards
  • We display all providers in search results — paid plans receive enhanced placement, not exclusive visibility (except City Sponsor slots)
