Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when listing a business (name, email, phone, address, service categories) or submitting an inquiry (name, email, phone, message). We also collect basic analytics data (page views, referrers) through Google Analytics.

How We Use Your Information

Provider information is displayed publicly in your directory listing. Homeowner inquiry information is shared exclusively with the provider you contact (paid listings only). We use email addresses to send listing confirmations, lead notifications, and occasional service updates.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Netlify's infrastructure. Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 and never stored in plain text. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Lead Routing

When a homeowner submits an inquiry, that inquiry is routed exclusively to the provider they selected (paid listings) or stored for future delivery (free listings). Leads are never shared with multiple providers simultaneously.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your listing or personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@homeservicedirectory.com. Providers can edit or remove their listings through the dashboard.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact hello@homeservicedirectory.com.
