Frequently Asked Questions

How does HomeServiceDirectory work?
HomeServiceDirectory connects homeowners with trusted, licensed service professionals across 12 categories including plumbing, HVAC, water damage restoration, mold remediation, and more. Search by service type and location to find verified providers with reviews, trust scores, and direct contact information.
Is HomeServiceDirectory free for homeowners?
Yes, HomeServiceDirectory is 100% free for homeowners. You can search, compare, read reviews, and contact service providers at no cost. Our revenue comes from service providers who pay for premium listing features.
How are service providers verified?
Service providers are scored on our Trust Score system which evaluates licensing, insurance, response time, customer reviews, profile completeness, and years in business. Verified providers display a green badge confirming their credentials have been checked.
What makes this different from Angi or Thumbtack?
Unlike Angi and Thumbtack which sell your lead to 3-5 competing companies, HomeServiceDirectory routes leads exclusively to one provider. Providers pay a flat monthly fee instead of per-lead charges, giving them predictable marketing costs and exclusive customer connections.
How do I list my business?
Listing is free and takes under 2 minutes. Visit our List Your Business page, fill out your company details, select your service categories, and your listing goes live instantly. Upgrade to Pro, Premium, Elite, or City Sponsor for enhanced features and exclusive lead access.
