Fire Damage Services

Explore the types of fire restoration services available through our directory

Fire Restoration

Complete fire damage restoration from initial emergency response through full rebuild. Includes structural assessment, demolition of unsalvageable materials, cleaning, deodorization, and reconstruction to return your property to pre-loss condition.

Smoke Damage

Smoke travels far beyond the fire area, leaving residue on walls, ceilings, ductwork, and contents throughout the home. Professional smoke damage cleaning uses specialized techniques for different residue types - dry smoke, wet smoke, protein smoke, and fuel oil smoke each require different approaches.

Soot Removal

Professional soot cleaning from walls, ceilings, woodwork, and contents using chemical sponges, HEPA vacuuming, and alkaline cleaning solutions. Soot is corrosive and acidic - delay in cleaning allows it to permanently etch and stain surfaces.

Board-Up Services

Emergency securing of the property after a fire including boarding up windows, doors, and roof openings. Prevents further damage from weather, vandalism, and animals. Roof tarping stops rain from compounding fire damage with water damage.

Content Cleaning

On-site and off-site cleaning of personal belongings, furniture, clothing, electronics, and documents affected by fire, smoke, and soot. Techniques include ultrasonic cleaning, ozone treatment, dry cleaning, and specialized electronics restoration.

Odor Removal

Professional smoke odor elimination using thermal fogging, ozone generators, hydroxyl generators, and air scrubbers. Smoke odor penetrates porous materials deeply - surface cleaning alone cannot eliminate it. Restoration companies treat the source, not just mask the smell.

Fire Damage FAQ

What should I do immediately after a house fire?
After the fire department clears the property: do not enter until given permission, contact your insurance company to start a claim, call a fire restoration company for emergency board-up and assessment, document all visible damage with photos and video, save all receipts for temporary housing and essentials, and do not attempt to clean soot or smoke damage yourself - improper cleaning can make damage permanent.
Does homeowner's insurance cover fire damage?
Yes, fire damage is one of the most comprehensively covered perils in standard homeowner's policies. Coverage typically includes structural repair/rebuild, personal property replacement, additional living expenses (hotel, meals, temporary rental), and debris removal. Your policy has coverage limits for each category. Work with a public adjuster or your restoration company to ensure your claim is fully documented.
How long does fire damage restoration take?
Timeline depends on severity. Minor kitchen fires with smoke damage may be restored in 1-2 weeks. Moderate fire damage requiring partial reconstruction takes 1-3 months. Major structural fires requiring extensive rebuild can take 6-12 months. Your restoration company should provide a detailed timeline after the initial assessment and keep you updated throughout the process.
Can smoke damage be fully removed?
Yes, professional restoration companies can fully remove smoke damage and odor in most cases. The key is acting quickly - soot becomes harder to remove as it sets, and smoke odor penetrates deeper over time. Porous materials like carpet, padding, and insulation may need replacement rather than cleaning. Hard surfaces, wood, and many contents can be restored to pre-loss condition with proper techniques.
Should I hire a restoration company or a general contractor?
For fire damage, hire a certified restoration company (IICRC-certified) first. They specialize in emergency response, smoke and soot chemistry, odor elimination, and content restoration that general contractors are not trained for. Many restoration companies also handle the rebuild phase, providing a single point of contact. If they do not rebuild, they can prepare the property properly before a general contractor begins reconstruction.
