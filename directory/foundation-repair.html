Foundation Services

Explore the types of foundation services available through our directory

Foundation Repair

Stabilization and leveling of settling or shifting foundations using push piers, helical piers, wall anchors, and carbon fiber reinforcement. Addresses cracked walls, sticking doors, uneven floors, and chimney separation caused by soil movement.

Basement Waterproofing

Interior and exterior waterproofing systems to stop basement water intrusion. Solutions include interior French drains, sump pump installation, exterior membrane waterproofing, crack injection, and downspout rerouting to keep basements dry.

Crawl Space Encapsulation

Full crawl space encapsulation seals the space with heavy-duty vapor barrier, installs a dehumidifier, and conditions the air to eliminate moisture, mold, wood rot, and pest issues. Improves indoor air quality and energy efficiency throughout the home.

Concrete Leveling

Polyurethane foam injection (mudjacking alternative) lifts and levels settled concrete slabs including driveways, sidewalks, garage floors, patios, and pool decks. Non-invasive, cures in minutes, and costs a fraction of full replacement.

Structural Engineering

Licensed structural engineers assess foundation damage, determine root causes, and design repair solutions. Engineering reports are required for insurance claims, real estate transactions, and ensuring repairs address the actual structural issue rather than just symptoms.

Pier Installation

Steel push piers and helical piers are driven to load-bearing strata beneath the foundation to provide permanent stabilization. Push piers use the home's weight to drive to depth, while helical piers are screwed into the soil and work on lighter structures.

Foundation Repair FAQ

What are the warning signs of foundation problems?
Key warning signs include cracks in walls (especially stair-step cracks in brick or diagonal cracks near windows and doors), doors and windows that stick or will not close properly, uneven or sloping floors, gaps between walls and ceiling or floor, chimney leaning or separating from the house, and water pooling near the foundation. A single hairline crack may be cosmetic, but multiple signs together indicate structural movement.
How much does foundation repair cost?
Foundation repair costs range from $2,000 for minor crack sealing to $7,000-$15,000 for pier installation on a standard home. Major structural repairs involving multiple piers, wall anchors, and excavation can reach $20,000-$40,000. Costs depend on the type of foundation, severity of damage, number of piers needed, accessibility, and soil conditions. Most companies offer free inspections and financing.
Does foundation repair affect home value?
Unrepaired foundation issues can reduce home value by 10-15% or more and scare away buyers. Professional foundation repair with a transferable warranty actually protects and can restore home value. Lenders require foundation issues to be resolved before approving mortgages. A repaired foundation with engineering documentation and a warranty gives buyers confidence.
How long does foundation repair take?
Most residential foundation repairs are completed in 1-3 days. A straightforward pier installation for one side of a home takes 1-2 days. Full-perimeter stabilization with interior work may take 3-5 days. Basement waterproofing systems take 2-4 days. The work is minimally invasive compared to what most homeowners expect, with small holes drilled for piers that are patched upon completion.
Does homeowner's insurance cover foundation repair?
Standard homeowner's insurance rarely covers foundation repair because most foundation damage is caused by soil settlement, expansive clay, or poor drainage - all considered gradual and excluded. However, if foundation damage results from a covered event like a plumbing leak or sudden ground collapse, some coverage may apply. Review your policy and consider a structural engineering report to support any claim.
