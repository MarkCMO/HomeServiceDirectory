Locksmith Services

Explore the types of locksmith services available through our directory

Emergency Lockout

24/7 residential lockout service when you are locked out of your home. Mobile locksmiths arrive with the tools to pick, bypass, or open your lock without damage. Most lockouts are resolved in 15-30 minutes without replacing the lock.

Lock Repair

Repair of malfunctioning deadbolts, door knobs, mortise locks, and smart locks. Common issues include stiff or stuck locks, keys that do not turn smoothly, broken internal mechanisms, and locks damaged by attempted break-ins. Repair is often faster and cheaper than full replacement.

Rekeying

Rekeying changes the internal pins of your existing locks so old keys no longer work, without replacing the entire lock hardware. Ideal after moving into a new home, losing keys, ending a roommate arrangement, or after a break-in. Much cheaper than replacing all your locks.

Lock Installation

Professional installation of deadbolts, smart locks, keypad locks, high-security locks, and multi-point locking systems. Includes door prep, strike plate reinforcement, and proper alignment for smooth operation and maximum security.

Security Systems

Security assessment and installation of complete access control systems including keypad entry, intercom systems, security cameras, door reinforcement hardware, and master key systems for multi-unit properties and businesses.

Safe Services

Safe opening for forgotten combinations, lock failures, and electronic malfunctions. Safe installation and bolting, combination changes, lock upgrades, and safe moving. Locksmiths can open most residential and commercial safes without drilling.

Car Lockout

Automotive lockout service for cars, trucks, and SUVs. Mobile locksmiths use specialized tools to open vehicle doors without damage. Also provides car key cutting, transponder key programming, key fob replacement, and ignition repair for most vehicle makes and models.

Commercial Locks

Commercial locksmith services for offices, retail stores, warehouses, and multi-tenant buildings. Includes high-security lock installation, master key systems, panic bar installation, keyless entry systems, and after-hours emergency lockout response for businesses.

Locksmith FAQ

How much does an emergency lockout service cost?
A standard residential lockout during business hours typically costs $50-$100. After-hours, weekend, and holiday calls usually run $75-$200. Car lockouts range from $50-$150 depending on the vehicle type and lock system. Be wary of extremely low quoted prices ($15-$25) - these are often bait-and-switch operations that inflate the price once they arrive. Always confirm the total cost before the locksmith begins work.
How do I avoid locksmith scams?
Locksmith scams are common. Protect yourself by verifying the locksmith has a local physical address (not just a phone number), asking for their license number before they arrive, confirming the price quote includes all fees (service call, labor, parts), checking that they arrive in a marked vehicle with company identification, and never agreeing to work that costs significantly more than the phone quote. Legitimate locksmiths provide written estimates.
Should I rekey or replace my locks when moving into a new home?
Always change or rekey locks when moving into a new home. You have no way of knowing how many copies of the keys exist from previous owners, realtors, contractors, or neighbors. Rekeying costs $15-$25 per lock and is the most cost-effective option if the existing hardware is in good condition. If the locks are old, low-quality, or you want smart lock features, full replacement ($75-$300 per lock installed) makes more sense.
Are smart locks more secure than traditional deadbolts?
High-quality smart locks from reputable brands (Schlage, Yale, August) are as secure as traditional Grade 1 or Grade 2 deadbolts for physical protection, with the added benefit of access logging, remote locking, and temporary codes for guests. However, they add potential attack vectors through Bluetooth, WiFi, or Z-Wave vulnerabilities. For maximum security, pair a smart lock with a traditional deadbolt as a backup.
Can a locksmith make a key without the original?
Yes. A skilled locksmith can create a new key by disassembling the lock and reading the pin configuration, by impressioning (inserting a blank and turning to create marks), or by decoding the lock. For cars, locksmiths can cut keys by VIN number using manufacturer databases and program transponder chips on-site. This is typically faster and cheaper than going through a car dealership for replacement keys.
