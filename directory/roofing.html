Roofing Services

Explore the types of roofing services available through our directory

Emergency Roof Repair

Immediate response for active roof leaks, storm damage, fallen trees, and structural failures. Emergency roofers provide temporary tarping and board-up to stop water intrusion, followed by permanent repair scheduling.

Roof Replacement

Full roof tear-off and replacement with asphalt shingles, metal roofing, tile, slate, or synthetic materials. Includes decking inspection and repair, ice and water shield, proper ventilation, and manufacturer warranty registration.

Leak Repair

Targeted leak repair for flashing failures, cracked boots around pipes, valley leaks, damaged shingles, and skylight leaks. Professional leak detection identifies the source, which is often different from where water appears inside.

Storm Damage

Storm damage assessment and repair for wind-lifted shingles, hail impact, fallen debris, and structural damage. Experienced roofers document damage for insurance claims and work directly with adjusters to maximize your coverage.

Gutter Installation

Seamless gutter installation, gutter guard systems, downspout extensions, and gutter repair. Properly functioning gutters protect your foundation, siding, and landscaping by directing water away from your home.

Roof Inspection

Comprehensive roof inspections for home purchases, insurance requirements, maintenance planning, and post-storm assessment. Includes attic inspection, exterior evaluation, flashing checks, and a detailed written report with photos.

Shingle Repair

Replacement of cracked, curling, missing, or wind-damaged shingles. Includes matching existing shingle color and style, replacing damaged underlayment, and sealing nail penetrations to restore weather protection.

Flat Roof

Flat and low-slope roofing installation and repair using TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, or built-up roofing. Flat roofs require specialized knowledge of drainage, seaming, and flashing details that differ from steep-slope systems.

Roofing FAQ

How much does a new roof cost?
A new asphalt shingle roof for an average-sized home (1,500-2,000 sq ft of roof area) typically costs $8,000-$15,000. Metal roofing runs $12,000-$25,000. Premium materials like slate or tile can exceed $30,000. Factors include roof complexity (hips, valleys, dormers), pitch, accessibility, decking condition, and local labor rates. Always get 3+ written estimates.
How do I know if I need a roof repair or full replacement?
Repair is appropriate for localized damage affecting less than 30% of the roof area, such as a few missing shingles or a single leak point. Replacement is recommended when shingles are curling or cracking across large areas, the roof is over 20 years old (for asphalt), there are multiple active leaks, or the decking shows widespread rot. A professional inspection can determine the best course of action.
Will my insurance cover roof storm damage?
Most homeowner's policies cover sudden storm damage including wind, hail, and fallen trees (minus your deductible). Some policies in hurricane or hail-prone areas have separate, higher wind/hail deductibles (often 1-2% of home value). Insurance does not cover damage from deferred maintenance or normal wear. File your claim promptly and have a roofing contractor present during the adjuster's inspection.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most residential roof replacements are completed in 1-3 days depending on the size and complexity of the roof, weather conditions, and the crew size. A simple single-story ranch can often be done in one day. Multi-story homes with complex roof lines, dormers, and steep pitches may take 3-5 days. Weather delays can extend the timeline.
What roofing material lasts the longest?
Slate roofing lasts 75-150 years. Clay and concrete tile last 50-100 years. Standing seam metal roofing lasts 40-70 years. Architectural (dimensional) asphalt shingles last 25-30 years, while 3-tab shingles last 15-20 years. Longer-lasting materials cost more upfront but often have a lower lifetime cost per year of service.
