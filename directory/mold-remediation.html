Mold Remediation Services

Explore the types of mold services available through our directory

Mold Inspection

Professional mold inspections include visual assessment, moisture mapping, thermal imaging, and identification of water intrusion sources. Inspectors document mold extent and provide a detailed remediation plan.

Mold Removal

Full remediation includes containment setup, HEPA air filtration, removal of contaminated materials, antimicrobial treatment, and post-remediation clearance testing to verify the space is safe.

Mold Testing

Air sampling and surface testing identify mold species and spore concentrations. Lab analysis determines whether Stachybotrys (black mold), Aspergillus, Penicillium, or other species are present and at what levels.

Black Mold

Stachybotrys chartarum (black mold) produces mycotoxins linked to respiratory problems and neurological symptoms. Remediation requires full containment, PPE, HEPA filtration, and removal of all colonized building materials.

Crawl Space Mold

Crawl spaces are prime mold habitats due to high humidity and poor ventilation. Remediation includes mold removal, vapor barrier installation, dehumidification, and encapsulation to prevent regrowth.

Attic Mold

Attic mold typically results from poor ventilation, bathroom exhaust fans venting into the attic, or roof leaks. Remediation includes mold treatment of rafters and sheathing, plus ventilation corrections to address the root cause.

Air Quality Testing

Indoor air quality testing measures mold spore counts, VOCs, humidity levels, and particulate matter. Baseline and post-remediation testing verifies that indoor air meets safe standards and remediation was effective.

Mold Remediation FAQ

How much does mold remediation cost?
Mold remediation costs typically range from $1,500 to $9,000 depending on the area affected, mold species, and accessibility. Small jobs (under 10 sq ft in accessible areas) may cost $500-$1,500. Large-scale remediation involving containment, demolition, and reconstruction can exceed $15,000. Always get multiple quotes and a written scope of work.
Is black mold dangerous? How do I know if I have it?
Black mold (Stachybotrys chartarum) produces mycotoxins that can cause respiratory issues, headaches, fatigue, and in severe cases neurological symptoms. However, you cannot identify mold species by color alone - many molds appear dark. Professional air sampling and lab analysis is the only reliable way to confirm which species are present in your home.
Does insurance cover mold remediation?
Homeowner's insurance may cover mold remediation if the mold resulted from a covered peril, such as a burst pipe. Mold from long-term neglect, poor maintenance, or flooding (without flood insurance) is typically excluded. Some policies have mold-specific caps of $5,000-$10,000. Check your policy and file a claim promptly if the mold source was a covered event.
Can I remove mold myself or do I need a professional?
The EPA recommends professional remediation for mold areas larger than 10 square feet. Small patches on non-porous surfaces (tile, glass) can be cleaned with detergent and water. Never use bleach on porous materials - it does not kill mold roots in wood or drywall. If you smell musty odors but cannot see mold, or if anyone in the home has health symptoms, hire a certified professional.
How long does mold remediation take?
A small mold remediation project (one room, surface mold) can be completed in 1-3 days. Medium projects involving multiple rooms or behind-wall mold take 3-7 days. Large-scale remediation with significant structural involvement can take 1-3 weeks. Post-remediation clearance testing is done 24-48 hours after completion to confirm success.
