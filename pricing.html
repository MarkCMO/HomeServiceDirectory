Pricing FAQ

Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately. There are no contracts or cancellation fees.
What does "exclusive leads" mean?
When a homeowner submits an inquiry through your listing, that lead is sent only to you. Unlike Angi and Thumbtack which sell the same lead to 3-5 competing businesses, your leads are 100% exclusive.
Do I need a separate listing for each service I offer?
No. One listing covers all service categories you select. If you do plumbing, water damage, and mold remediation, your single listing appears in all three directories. Premium and above get leads across all categories.
How does City Sponsor work?
City Sponsor gives you the exclusive #1 position for your chosen city and service category. Only one sponsor is allowed per city/category combination. When a homeowner searches for your service in your city, you appear first - guaranteed. Contact us for availability and pricing.
Why don't free listings receive leads?
Free listings give you visibility on the directory and in search engines. Leads are stored in our system, but they are only forwarded to paid members. This ensures our paying providers get the highest quality, exclusive leads.
